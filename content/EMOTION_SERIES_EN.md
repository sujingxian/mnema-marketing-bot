# TikTok Emotion Series · 60 Posts (EN)

> Framework (emotion → real need → what it's actually saying → common misread → one action)
> is standard psychoeducation. Wording here is original — do NOT translate the Chinese
> reference table cell-by-cell. That's someone else's copy.
>
> Not clinical advice. No diagnosis language. No pop-neuroscience ("your amygdala…").

## Script skeleton (15–22s)

```
HOOK   2s  the misread, in the viewer's own words
TURN   2s  reject it
NAME   4s  the real emotion + the need under it
LINE   5s  the sentence the emotion is actually saying
DO     5s  one thing doable in 60 seconds
LOOP   2s  optional — makes them rewatch or comment
```

## Hook rotation — cycle A→C→D→B→E, never two of the same in a row

- **A** misread flip — "You call it laziness. Your body calls it running out."
- **B** sentence completion — "Anxiety isn't asking *what if*. It's asking *can I survive it*."
- **C** scene call-out — "If your jaw's been tight since Monday—"
- **D** correct a phrase — "Stop saying you're too sensitive."
- **E** question — "Why does guilt show up when you did nothing wrong?"

## Visual system

- **Base format**: black card, one word per beat. Misread word → (cut) real emotion → (cut) the LINE.
- **Every 5th post**: handwritten note, hand in frame, writing the LINE. Proof of a human.
- **Last 2s**: real Mnema screenshot with that emotion tagged. Never fabricate one.
- **Cluster colors**: Fear `#4A7EC0` · Shame `#A85455` · Confusion `#6E6A8C` · Sadness `#3E4C7A` · Anger `#B5663C`
- **Never**: rain-on-window, slow push-in on plants, AI sad girl, drone + piano, stock knees-to-chest.

## Safety

`#43 Despair` and `#45 Grief` — do NOT run the tip format. No DO beat. End with
"this one isn't a tip" + local helpline card. Check TikTok's current policy before posting.

# CLUSTER 1 · FEAR & ANXIETY  `#4A7EC0`

### 01 Fear — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're a coward.
- **TURN** You're not. You're unsure you can handle it.
- **NAME** Fear wants safety, not courage.
- **LINE** "I don't know if I can survive this" is a different sentence from "I'm weak."
- **DO** Name the first step only. Not the plan. The step.
- **SCREEN** COWARD → UNSURE → "Can I handle it?"

### 02 Anxiety — hook B
- **HOOK** Anxiety isn't asking "what if." It's asking "could I handle it if it happened."
- **TURN** That's not weakness.
- **NAME** It's a prediction engine running with no data.
- **LINE** It needs certainty. You've been giving it more questions.
- **DO** Write the worst case in one sentence. Not a feeling — a sentence.
- **LOOP** A blur can't be checked. A sentence can.

### 03 Nervousness — hook C
- **HOOK** If your hands go cold before you speak—
- **TURN** that's not incompetence.
- **NAME** Nerves want preparation. They show up because you care how this goes.
- **LINE** "I want to do this well" wearing a costume.
- **DO** Rehearse the first 30 seconds. Only that. The rest runs on momentum.

### 04 Unease — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop calling yourself too sensitive.
- **TURN** Unease isn't sensitivity.
- **NAME** It's needing steady ground and not having it.
- **LINE** "I'm braced for something to be taken."
- **DO** Name the one specific thing you're bracing for. Vague dread can't be argued with.

### 05 Worry — hook E
- **HOOK** Why can't you stop thinking about it?
- **TURN** Because it's not filed.
- **NAME** Worry wants a plan. Not reassurance — a plan.
- **LINE** "I can't tell if this is handled."
- **DO** Give it a time and a next action, or write "not mine." Both close the loop.

### 06 Dread — hook A
- **HOOK** You think something's wrong with you.
- **TURN** Something was wrong. Past tense.
- **NAME** Dread wants protection. It filed this situation next to an old one.
- **LINE** "I've been here before and it went badly."
- **DO** Ask one question: is the danger now, or is it a memory? Say the answer out loud.

### 07 Fluster — hook C
- **HOOK** If everything feels urgent and nothing's actually on fire—
- **TURN** you're not overreacting.
- **NAME** Too many inputs, all at once. Fluster wants slower.
- **LINE** "I can't sort this fast enough."
- **DO** Close every tab but one. Physically. It works because the mess was real.

### 08 Panic — hook D
- **HOOK** Don't tell yourself to calm down. It has never once worked.
- **TURN** Panic isn't asking for calm.
- **NAME** It's asking for an anchor. Something that says *here, now, solid*.
- **LINE** "I've lost my footing."
- **DO** Grab something cold. Name what's under your feet. Out loud, if you can.

### 09 Worrying about the future — hook B
- **HOOK** You're not overthinking the future. You're trying to see a path that isn't drawn yet.
- **TURN** That's not a thinking problem.
- **NAME** It wants direction, and you're demanding a destination.
- **LINE** "I don't know where this goes."
- **DO** Decide the next seven days. That's the whole assignment.

### 10 Tension — hook C
- **HOOK** If your jaw's been tight since Monday and you can't say why—
- **TURN** that's not stress.
- **NAME** That's a body that never got the all-clear.
- **LINE** On guard so long that guarding stopped feeling like anything.
- **DO** Unclench your jaw. Drop your shoulders. Count how long they stay down.
- **LOOP** That number is your answer.

### 11 Heaviness — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you have to carry it.
- **TURN** Some of it was never handed to you. You picked it up.
- **NAME** Heaviness wants the load reduced, not endured better.
- **LINE** "If I put this down, something breaks."
- **DO** List what's on you. Cross out what isn't yours. Look at what's left.

### 12 Frazzled — hook E
- **HOOK** Why does a normal week suddenly feel impossible?
- **TURN** Nothing got harder.
- **NAME** It lost its shape. Frazzled wants order, not more effort.
- **LINE** "I can't tell what comes first."
- **DO** Write the three things. Do the top one. Ignore that the list is incomplete.

# CLUSTER 2 · SHAME & SELF-WORTH  `#A85455`

### 13 Shame — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're bad.
- **TURN** You did something you regret. Those aren't the same sentence.
- **NAME** Shame wants to still belong.
- **LINE** "If they knew, they'd leave."
- **DO** Say it as "I did X." Not "I am X." Notice the difference in your chest.

### 14 Inferiority — hook C
- **HOOK** If you open one app and feel behind in nine seconds—
- **TURN** you're not behind.
- **NAME** You compared your inside to their outside. Everyone loses that one.
- **LINE** "Everyone got the manual and I didn't."
- **DO** Write one thing you handled this week that nobody saw.

### 15 Guilt — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop punishing yourself and calling it accountability.
- **TURN** Guilt isn't asking for a sentence.
- **NAME** It's asking for repair. It shows up because you care about the thing you broke.
- **LINE** "I broke something that mattered to me."
- **DO** Name the repair. Then do the smallest version of it today.

### 16 Self-blame — hook B
- **HOOK** Self-blame isn't honesty. It's a standard set for someone with unlimited energy.
- **TURN** You didn't have unlimited energy.
- **NAME** It wants relief and is asking for a verdict instead.
- **LINE** "I should have done better with what I had."
- **DO** Say to yourself, out loud, what you'd say to a friend. Out loud matters.

### 17 Humiliation — hook E
- **HOOK** Why does one comment stay for six years?
- **TURN** Because it didn't land on what you did.
- **NAME** It landed on who you are. Humiliation wants that separated again.
- **LINE** "They treated one moment as my whole character."
- **DO** Put the moment back where it belongs: one thing, one day, not you.

### 18 Embarrassment — hook A
- **HOOK** You think everyone saw.
- **TURN** They saw for four seconds and went back to their own day.
- **NAME** Embarrassment wants dignity restored. It overestimates the audience.
- **LINE** "That's the thing they'll remember about me."
- **DO** Ask yourself what someone else did last Tuesday that embarrassed them. Exactly. That's how much they remember yours.

### 19 Regret — hook C
- **HOOK** If you keep replaying one decision at 2am—
- **TURN** that's not self-torture.
- **NAME** Regret wants a redo. It only shows up for things you actually wanted.
- **LINE** "I want it back."
- **DO** Ask what version of that is still available now. Usually something is.

### 20 Remorse — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop saying you don't deserve to be happy.
- **TURN** That's a sentence you handed yourself with no trial.
- **NAME** Remorse wants release, not a life term.
- **LINE** "I can't forgive myself for that."
- **DO** Write what you won't repeat. That's the only part of the past you can still touch.

### 21 Self-doubt — hook B
- **HOOK** Self-doubt isn't proof you can't. It's proof you haven't yet.
- **TURN** Those feel identical from the inside. They're not.
- **NAME** It wants evidence.
- **LINE** "I don't know if I've got this."
- **DO** List three times you also didn't know, and it worked anyway.

### 22 Self-negation — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're worthless.
- **TURN** Your scoring system is broken. That's a different repair job.
- **NAME** It wants a fair judge. Right now the judge only counts misses.
- **LINE** "Nothing I do counts."
- **DO** Count one thing that counted today. Out loud. It'll feel stupid. Do it anyway.

### 23 Self-consciousness — hook C
- **HOOK** If you rehearse a sentence four times before saying it in a group—
- **TURN** you're not awkward.
- **NAME** You're scanning for judgment. It wants to feel safe enough to be unpolished.
- **LINE** "If I get this wrong they'll see something about me."
- **DO** Come back to your body. Feet on the floor. The scan runs in your head, not the room.

### 24 Caring what others think — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop saying you care too much what people think.
- **TURN** You don't care too much.
- **NAME** You handed someone else the scoreboard.
- **LINE** "I'll know if this was good when they tell me."
- **DO** Take one thing this week and score it yourself. Before you ask anyone.

# CLUSTER 3 · CONFUSION & NUMBNESS  `#6E6A8C`

### 25 Torn — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're indecisive.
- **TURN** You want two things and can have one. Everyone stalls there.
- **NAME** Torn wants a clear call, not more options.
- **LINE** "Whatever I pick, I lose something."
- **DO** Say out loud what you're giving up. Then choose. The grief is the price, not a warning.

### 26 Conflicted — hook B
- **HOOK** Being conflicted isn't confusion. It's two true things refusing to merge.
- **TURN** You're not unstable.
- **NAME** It wants integration, not a winner.
- **LINE** "I love it and I want out."
- **DO** Let both stay true for a week. Stop forcing a verdict.

### 27 Lost — hook E
- **HOOK** Why does "I don't know what I'm doing" feel like a character flaw?
- **TURN** It isn't one.
- **NAME** No path is not the same as no ability. Lost wants direction.
- **LINE** "Everyone else has a map."
- **DO** Find the smallest step that's true regardless of where this ends up. Take that one.

### 28 Weariness — hook C
- **HOOK** If something you used to love feels flat now—
- **TURN** you haven't gone cold.
- **NAME** The same input stopped registering. Weariness wants change, not discipline.
- **LINE** "I should still want this."
- **DO** Change one variable. Same thing, different room, different time, different order.

### 29 Numbness — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're broken.
- **TURN** Your system turned the volume down. On purpose. To protect you.
- **NAME** Numbness wants to feel again, slowly.
- **LINE** "I don't feel anything and that scares me more."
- **DO** Start physical, not emotional. Temperature, texture, weight. Big feelings come back last.

### 30 Blankness — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop calling it a lack of willpower.
- **TURN** Blank isn't empty. It's disconnected.
- **NAME** It wants a reboot, and thinking harder is not one.
- **LINE** "There's nothing in there."
- **DO** Do something with your hands for ten minutes. The line back in is physical.

### 31 Dazed — hook B
- **HOOK** You're not lacking direction. You're lacking criteria.
- **TURN** Different problem, different fix.
- **NAME** Dazed wants bearings — a way to tell good from bad.
- **LINE** "Every option looks the same."
- **DO** Pick the criterion first. Then the options sort themselves.

### 32 Chaos — hook C
- **HOOK** If your life feels like a mess but you can't say which part—
- **TURN** it's probably not your life.
- **NAME** It's that it all lives in your head at once. Chaos wants it externalized.
- **LINE** "Everything is everywhere."
- **DO** One page. Everything on it. Don't organize yet — just get it out.

### 33 Rumination — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're overthinking.
- **TURN** You're running two directions at once.
- **NAME** Rumination isn't a thinking problem. It's an unmade decision burning fuel.
- **LINE** "I'll decide once I'm sure."
- **DO** Pick one. Today. Even the worse one.
- **LOOP** You can survive a wrong turn. You can't out-think a fork.

### 34 Fragility — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop calling yourself dramatic.
- **TURN** Fragile isn't a personality. It's a level.
- **NAME** Resources are low. It wants support, not toughening up.
- **LINE** "Normal things are landing harder than usual."
- **DO** Ask one person for one specific thing. Specific. "Support" isn't a request.

### 35 Detachment — hook E
- **HOOK** Why did you go cold on someone you used to care about?
- **TURN** You didn't become a worse person.
- **NAME** You shut a door after too much came through it. Detachment wants recovery.
- **LINE** "I don't have anything left for this."
- **DO** Let the door stay shut for a while. It reopens on its own more often than you'd think.

### 36 Rejection — hook A
- **HOOK** You think it means you're not worth it.
- **TURN** One no is one no. It isn't a ruling.
- **NAME** Rejection wants to still belong somewhere.
- **LINE** "This is evidence about me."
- **DO** Write what it's actually evidence of. Usually: one person, one moment, one fit.

# CLUSTER 4 · SADNESS & DEPLETION  `#3E4C7A`

### 37 Sadness — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop telling yourself you shouldn't be sad about this.
- **TURN** Sadness doesn't check whether the loss was big enough.
- **NAME** It wants time and permission.
- **LINE** "Something's gone."
- **DO** Give it an hour where you're not trying to fix it. Sadness moves when it isn't rushed.

### 38 Letdown — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you're being dramatic.
- **TURN** You're disappointed because you actually cared. That's the whole mechanism.
- **NAME** Letdown wants expectations recalibrated, not feelings suppressed.
- **LINE** "I hoped for more than I admitted."
- **DO** Say what you were actually hoping for. You probably never said it out loud.

### 39 Ache — hook C
- **HOOK** If you did everything right and nobody noticed—
- **TURN** that ache isn't self-pity.
- **NAME** It wants to be seen. Effort that goes unwitnessed hurts in a specific way.
- **LINE** "I worked for that and it disappeared."
- **DO** Tell one person what you did. Not for praise. For a witness.

### 40 Feeling wronged — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop calling silence maturity.
- **TURN** Swallowing it isn't strength. It's just quiet.
- **NAME** This wants to be understood correctly. You were misread and said nothing.
- **LINE** "That's not what happened and nobody asked."
- **DO** Say the correction. Calm, short, once. "That's not how it went for me."

### 41 Powerlessness — hook A
- **HOOK** You call it laziness. Your body calls it running out.
- **TURN** Powerless isn't a character flaw. It's a resource report.
- **NAME** It wants rest and limits.
- **LINE** "The tank is empty and I'm still asking why the car won't move."
- **DO** Rest before you've earned it. That's the only time it actually helps.

### 42 Loneliness — hook B
- **HOOK** Loneliness isn't a lack of people. It's a lack of being known.
- **TURN** That's why a full room doesn't fix it.
- **NAME** It wants resonance, not company.
- **LINE** "Nobody here knows what my week was."
- **DO** Tell one person one true thing. Small is fine. Known beats surrounded.

- **TURN** despair isn't a conclusion. It's a symptom of a field of view that's collapsed.
- **NAME** It wants possibility back, and it can't generate that alone.
- **LINE** "There's nothing ahead."

### 44 Emotional exhaustion — hook C
- **HOOK** If you slept nine hours and still feel like this—
- **TURN** it isn't a sleep problem.
- **NAME** You've been holding a face on for months. It wants a pause, not a nap.
- **LINE** "I have to keep it together."
- **DO** Find one place today where you don't perform anything. Fifteen minutes.

### 45 Grief — SAFETY-ADJACENT · soft version
- **HOOK** They tell you to be strong.
- **TURN** Grief isn't a strength test.
- **NAME** It wants room. It gets bigger when it's kept quiet.
- **LINE** "If I start I won't stop."
- **DO** Let it run once, on purpose, somewhere safe. It has an end. Holding it doesn't.
- **NOTE** No "steps." No timeline. Do not imply it should be over.

### 46 Emptiness — hook E
- **HOOK** Why does everything feel pointless when nothing's wrong?
- **TURN** Emptiness isn't ingratitude.
- **NAME** It's disconnection from what mattered. It wants meaning, not stimulation.
- **LINE** "I'm doing everything and none of it lands."
- **DO** One small thing that means something. Not one big thing. Small survives.

### 47 Wistfulness — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop calling yourself too nostalgic.
- **TURN** Wistful isn't living in the past.
- **NAME** It's unfinished business with it. It wants integration.
- **LINE** "Something back there isn't closed."
- **DO** Write what you want to keep from it. Keeping is allowed. Only returning isn't possible.

### 48 Melancholy — hook B
- **HOOK** Melancholy isn't sadness. It's memory being processed.
- **TURN** That's maintenance, not malfunction.
- **NAME** It wants review, then release.
- **LINE** "I'm somewhere else for a while."
- **DO** Let it run for one song. Then get up. Visit, don't move in.

# CLUSTER 5 · ANGER & BOUNDARIES  `#B5663C`

### 49 Anger — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you have a temper.
- **TURN** A line got crossed. Anger is the alarm, not the crime.
- **NAME** It wants respect and a boundary.
- **LINE** "That wasn't okay and I said nothing."
- **DO** Name the specific line. Not "they're awful" — which line, exactly.

### 50 Rage — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop calling yourself too emotional.
- **TURN** Rage shows up when something is unfair. It's usually accurate.
- **NAME** It wants fairness. What it does next is a separate decision.
- **LINE** "This is wrong and nobody's stopping it."
- **DO** Separate feeling it from acting on it. You can be right and still choose the timing.

### 51 Irritability — hook C
- **HOOK** If everyone's annoying today and yesterday they weren't—
- **TURN** it's not them.
- **NAME** Too much input. Irritability wants less, not patience.
- **LINE** "One more thing and I'm done."
- **DO** Leave the room for five minutes. Physically. Willpower isn't the fix here.

### 52 Dissatisfaction — hook B
- **HOOK** Dissatisfaction isn't being picky. It's a gap between this and what you value.
- **TURN** That's information.
- **NAME** It wants change, and it'll keep talking until it gets specifics.
- **LINE** "This isn't how it should be."
- **DO** Turn it into one specific request. Complaints repeat. Requests resolve.

### 53 Feeling stifled — hook E
- **HOOK** Why does holding it in make it worse instead of smaller?
- **TURN** Because it wasn't asking to be held.
- **NAME** It wants expression. Pressure doesn't decay on its own.
- **LINE** "There's no version of saying this that goes well."
- **DO** Say the sentence out loud, alone, once. The first time is always the hardest one.

### 54 Aversion — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you've become unkind.
- **TURN** You're out of capacity for one specific person.
- **NAME** Aversion wants distance and energy back.
- **LINE** "I can't keep giving this one anything."
- **DO** Reduce contact before you resent them. Distance early beats damage later.

### 55 Impulsiveness — hook D
- **HOOK** Stop saying you have no self-control.
- **TURN** You have a fast system and a slow one, and the fast one gets there first.
- **NAME** It wants delay, not discipline.
- **LINE** "I need this handled right now."
- **DO** Sixty seconds. That's it. That's the whole technique and it works.

### 56 Resentment — hook C
- **HOOK** If you're keeping a running tally of what you've done for someone—
- **TURN** you're not petty.
- **NAME** You've been giving more than you ever said out loud. Resentment wants fairness.
- **LINE** "They should know by now."
- **DO** They don't know. Say the specific ask. Tally is what happens instead of asking.

### 57 Envy — hook E
- **HOOK** Why does envy feel like being a bad person?
- **TURN** It isn't. Envy is a map.
- **NAME** It only points at things you actually want.
- **LINE** "They have the thing I'm not admitting I want."
- **DO** Name the one specific thing. Not their life — the thing. That's a goal in disguise.

### 58 Contempt — hook A
- **HOOK** You think you've gotten arrogant.
- **TURN** You've found something you don't want to become.
- **NAME** Contempt wants separation. It goes bad when it turns into attack.
- **LINE** "I refuse to live like that."
- **DO** Draw the line without the commentary. Distance is the point, not damage.

### 59 Annoyance — hook C
- **HOOK** If a normal text made you furious today—
- **TURN** the text isn't the problem.
- **NAME** You're overstimulated. Annoyance wants the input reduced first.
- **LINE** "Everything is too loud."
- **DO** Cut input before you respond. Reply to nothing for thirty minutes.

### 60 Disgust — hook B
- **HOOK** Disgust isn't judgment. It's your body refusing something.
- **TURN** It's older and faster than your reasoning.
- **NAME** It wants you out of the situation.
- **LINE** "I need to not be here."
- **DO** Trust it first, analyze later. This one is usually right before you can explain why.
